# Introducing Lumen: Instant, Intelligent Search for Everything You’ve Ever Noted

Taking notes is easy. Finding them six months later—when you vaguely remember an idea, a decision, or a customer quote, but cannot recall the exact keywords you used—is painful.

Today, we are thrilled to introduce **Lumen**, a brand-new AI-powered search experience built directly into your notes. Lumen moves beyond rigid keyword matching, allowing you to search by concept, ask natural-language questions, and effortlessly connect the dots across your entire digital knowledge base.

---

## Why We Built Lumen

Over the past few years, the way we capture information has exploded. Meeting transcripts, project specs, quick brain-dumps, research snippets, and personal journals all live side by side in your workspace. 

However, traditional search hasn't kept up. Keyword search requires you to remember the exact phrasing you used when writing. If you search for "pricing strategy" but wrote "subscription tiers update," standard search comes up empty. You waste valuable time clicking through folders or scanning lengthy documents.

We built Lumen to bridge the gap between how you think and how your notes are organized.

---

## What Lumen Can Do

### 1. Conceptual and Natural Language Search
You don't need to guess exact tags or keywords anymore. Search the way you speak:
- *"What did Sarah recommend for the onboarding redesign?"*
- *"Key takeaways from our conversation with Acme Corp last quarter"*
- *"Ideas on reducing churn from last month's offsite"*

Lumen understands the underlying intent and semantic meaning behind your queries, surfacing the right notes even when phrasing differs completely.

### 2. Instant Answers with Direct Citations
When you're in the middle of deep work, you don't always have time to read through three separate five-page meeting notes. 

When you ask a question, Lumen generates a concise synthesis summarizing the answer across your notes, paired with clickable citations directly to the source paragraphs. You get the quick summary you need, with the confidence that you can verify the original source with a single click.

### 3. Cross-Notebook Connections
Lumen constantly maps conceptual relationships between your notes. When viewing a project brief, Lumen can surface relevant research notes, past post-mortems, or related team memos you may have completely forgotten existed.

### 4. Search Across Formats
Lumen indexes more than plain text. It extracts and understands content within PDFs, code blocks, screenshots, and audio transcriptions, making your entire archive searchable in one unified view.

---

## Privacy by Design

Your notes represent your most personal thoughts, private sketches, and proprietary business knowledge. We designed Lumen with a privacy-first foundation:

- **Zero Data Training**: Your notes are never used to train foundational AI models.
- **Enterprise-Grade Encryption**: All search indexing and queries are encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Granular Permissions**: Lumen strictly respects existing permission boundaries. Team members will only ever see search results and summaries from documents they already have access to.

## Getting Started

Lumen is rolling out starting today to all desktop and web users, with mobile support arriving in the coming weeks.

To try Lumen:
1. Open the search bar using `Cmd + K` (macOS) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows/Linux).
2. Toggle to **Lumen Search** or simply ask a question in natural language.
3. Explore the synthesized answers and linked references.

Lumen is included in all Pro and Team workspaces at no additional cost during the preview period.
