## Features

### ⏱ Tracking that fits how designers work
Start a timer in one click from your menu bar, browser, or phone. If you forget, Tally notices you've been in a client's Figma file for 40 minutes and asks whether to log it.

### 🗂 Sorted by client and project
Every hour goes into the right client, project, and phase: discovery, concepts, revisions, handoff. You'll see where your time actually goes, including the "quick tweaks" that took six hours.

### 🔁 Revision tracking
Tag time as a revision round and Tally counts how many you've delivered against what's in the contract. When a client asks for round four on a three-round scope, you'll have the numbers ready.

### 💸 Invoices in a few clicks
Turn tracked hours into a clean, branded invoice. Choose hourly, fixed-fee, or retainer billing, add expenses, and send. Clients can pay by card or bank transfer from the invoice itself.

### 📊 Know your real rate
On a fixed-fee project, Tally divides what you earned by the hours you put in and shows your effective hourly rate. That helps you price the next project.

### 🎯 Budget alerts
Set an hour or fee budget for each project. Tally warns you at 75% and 90%, so you can talk to the client before you're working for free.

### 🔒 Private by default
Tally logs which app and file you're in. It doesn't take screenshots or log keystrokes. Your data stays yours, and you choose what clients see.

## FAQ

**Do I have to remember to start a timer?**
No. Timers are there if you like them. If you don't, Tally's activity detection suggests time entries from your work, and you approve them with a tap.

**Will my clients see everything I track?**
Only if you choose to share it. Your timeline stays private. Clients see the reports and invoices you send them.

**Which tools does Tally work with?**
Tally detects activity in Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Framer, Sketch, and any browser. It also exports to QuickBooks, Xero, and CSV.

**Can I use Tally for fixed-fee projects?**
Yes. Tracking time on fixed-fee work shows your effective hourly rate, so you can tell which projects pay well and which ones cost you.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Tally keeps tracking without a connection and syncs once you're back online.

**Is my data secure?**
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell it, and you can export or delete everything whenever you want.

**What happens when my trial ends?**
If you don't upgrade, your account moves to the Free plan. All your tracked time stays where it is.

**Can I import data from another time tracker?**
Yes. You can import from Toggl, Harvest, Clockify, or any CSV in a few minutes.
